Get booked. Get signed. Get paid.

Slate Pro is the booking page for photographers, videographers and creators, with a contract in every booking.

What it does

  • One link to share. Clients pick a package and a time that is actually free.
  • Contracts built in. Every booking is signed before it is confirmed.
  • Deposits up front. Clients pay with Apple Pay; you are paid through Stripe.
  • Deliver in the app. Send photos and videos, let clients preview them, and unlock the originals when the balance is paid.
  • For one person or a whole studio. Companies can add their team, share services and assign bookings.

Slate Pro is in private beta. It runs on iPhone through TestFlight. To ask for an invitation, email support@slateproapp.com.

Looking for a creator’s booking page? Open the link they sent you in Slate Pro. Web booking pages are coming later.
