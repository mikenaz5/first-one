Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what Slate Pro (“we”) collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. Slate Pro is operated by [[LEGAL NAME OF THE COMPANY OR PERSON]], [[ADDRESS]]. Questions: support@slateproapp.com.

What we collect

Your account

Your name and email address, from Sign in with Apple (which may be a private relay address) or the address you type for an email sign-in code.

Your profile, if you are a creator

Your link name, display name, bio, specialties, city and travel distance, profile photo, portfolio photos and videos, and your packages and prices. These appear on your public page.

Bookings and contracts

The package, date and time, notes, the contract text, the names and signatures of the people who sign (typed or drawn), and the signed PDF. Both people on a booking can see them.

Payments

Amounts, dates and status of deposits, balances and refunds. Payments are handled by Stripe and Apple Pay. We never see or store a full card number. If you save a card, Stripe keeps it; we keep only its brand, last four digits, expiry and a reference only our servers can use.

A company’s plan is bought with Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple takes that payment and tells us the plan’s status and renewal date; we never see the payment method.

What Stripe’s software in the app collects

To take a payment with Apple Pay or to save a card, the app uses software from Stripe. That software may collect information about your device (for example its model, operating system version, language, time zone and an identifier that Stripe creates for the device) and about how the payment screens are used (for example which steps are reached, how long they take and any errors), and sends it to Stripe, not to our servers. Stripe uses it to detect and prevent fraud and to keep and improve its own software, under Stripe’s privacy policy. It is not used for advertising or to follow you across other companies’ apps and websites. We leave Stripe’s fraud-prevention signals switched on because they help protect creators from payments made with stolen cards.

Messages

What you and the other person on a booking write to each other.

Photos and videos you deliver or receive

Stored privately, visible only to the two people on the booking (and, for a company, the team members whose role allows it). The creator’s files are deleted 30 days after delivery, or sooner if the creator removes them.

Notifications

A token that lets Apple deliver notifications to your phone, and your notification choices.

Calendar, only if you turn it on

If you connect your calendar, only the start and end times of your busy events are sent, never titles or details. Bookings can also be added to your calendar.

Location, only if you allow it

If you allow it, your phone finds your approximate location once to show creators near you. It is rounded to about a kilometre, kept on your phone, and not stored on our servers.

Reports

What you write when you report a file.

Technical information

Our hosting providers keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and time of a request) for security and reliability. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your information. If you share crash reports or analytics with Apple through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may give us a summary.

How we use it

  • To run the service: bookings, contracts, payments, deliveries, messages and notifications.
  • To keep it safe: preventing fraud and abuse, and investigating reports.
  • To meet legal obligations and to answer your requests.

Who sees it

  • The other person on a booking sees what is needed to complete it: names, booking details, the contract, messages and delivered files.
  • Anyone can see a creator’s public page.
  • Our service providers, only to run Slate Pro: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and server functions), Stripe (payments, creator payout accounts and saved cards, including the software described above; Stripe may verify a creator’s identity itself) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, Apple Pay, the company plan subscription and notifications).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your account in the app (Settings › Delete account). That removes your profile, portfolio, delivered files and sign-in, and deletes your saved cards at Stripe. Contracts, bookings, payment records and messages that involve another person stay available to that person, because they are their records too. We also keep records we are required by law to keep, such as payment records. Delivered files are always deleted 30 days after delivery.

Your choices

You can see and edit your profile in the app, change notification and calendar settings in the app and in iOS Settings, and turn off location in iOS Settings. To ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it, email support@slateproapp.com. [[Add the rights that apply where you operate, for example California, the UK or the EU.]]

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Who may see which booking, contract, message or file is enforced in our database, not only in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

Slate Pro is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
