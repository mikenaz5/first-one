Support

Email support@slateproapp.com and tell us what happened. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Please never send a card number or a password. We will never ask for one.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in with Apple, or choose “Continue with email” and we will email you a six-digit code. There is no password.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile › Settings (clients: Profile) and choose Delete account…. If you own a company, hand it to someone else or close it first. What is removed and what stays is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel a booking? Will I get my money back?

Open the booking and choose Cancel. The cancellation policy you agreed to when you booked decides the refund, and the screen shows the exact amount before you confirm. Refunds go back to the card that paid.

I paid, but my photos are still locked.

Originals unlock the moment Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds. Close and reopen the booking, or pull down on its photos and videos page to refresh. If it is still locked after a few minutes, email us the booking date and your creator’s name.

How long do delivered photos and videos stay?

Thirty days from the day they are delivered, then they are deleted. Slate Pro is not an archive, so save your files to Photos or Files before the date shown on the booking. We remind you 7 days and 1 day before.

How do I report a file or a person?

Open the file and choose More › Report this file, or email us with the booking details. For anything illegal or urgent, say so in the subject line.

I am a creator. How do I get paid?

Connect a Stripe account from your profile or Home screen. Payments go straight from the client to your Stripe account; Slate Pro does not hold your money.

I run a studio. How do seats work?

A company plan is billed per seat through Stripe, with a minimum number of seats. The Owner can see and change the plan in Settings › Billing.
